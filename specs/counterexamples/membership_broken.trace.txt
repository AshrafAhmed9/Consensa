Root cause: the broken HasQuorum uses OR (majority of Old \/ majority of New) during the
joint phase instead of AND. {n1,n2} and {n3,n4} are disjoint, so nothing forces them to
share a voter -- the single-vote-per-term rule only prevents double-voting, it cannot by
itself prevent two non-overlapping quorums from existing when the rule accepts either one
alone. The dual-majority AND requirement in membership.tla is what forces every majority
of Old to intersect every majority of New (pigeonhole on the shared members), which is
the actual safety argument -- and membership.tla passes with the same nodes and votes.
